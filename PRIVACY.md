# Privacy Policy — social-autopost

social-autopost is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish their own videos to their own Instagram and YouTube channels on a schedule.

- **What it accesses:** only the owner's own Instagram and YouTube accounts, with permission to upload videos and read basic channel details.
- **What it stores:** nothing about other people. Login tokens are kept as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never shared or published.
- **Who uses it:** only the owner. It is not offered to the public and collects no data from viewers or other users.
- **Sharing:** no data is sold or shared with third parties.
- **Revoking access:** the owner can remove access at any time from their Google account (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Meta business settings.

Use of YouTube data follows the [YouTube API Services Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Contact: open an issue on this repository.
